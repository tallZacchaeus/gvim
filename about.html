About Us

Discover our heart, mission, and the people who make GVIM a place of worship and fellowship

Our Vision

To see every believer equipped and empowered as God's vessel, transforming communities through the love and power of Jesus Christ, making disciples of all nations.

Our Mission

To worship God in spirit and truth, teach His Word with clarity and conviction, and serve our community with compassion, raising up mature believers who impact the world for Christ.

Our Values

Faith, Love, Unity, Excellence, and Service guide everything we do as we strive to honor God and serve others with integrity and purpose.

Our Ministry Pillars

Worship

We believe in the power of authentic worship that touches hearts and transforms lives. Our worship services are designed to create an atmosphere where people can encounter God's presence.

Word

The Word of God is our foundation. We are committed to teaching the Bible with accuracy, relevance, and practical application for daily living.

Fellowship

We foster genuine relationships and community through small groups, ministry teams, and church-wide events that build lasting connections.

Service

Following Christ's example, we serve our community through outreach programs, community service, and missions both locally and globally.

Discipleship

We are committed to helping believers grow in their faith through mentoring, training, and equipping programs that develop spiritual maturity.

Evangelism

Sharing the Gospel is at the heart of our mission. We actively reach out to our community and beyond with the message of hope and salvation.

Our Lead Pastor

Rev. Godwin BB. Olutimi

Lead Pastor/Reverend

Currently serving as the lead Pastor/Reverend in God's Vessels International Ministry (GVIM), Pastor Godwin has been ministering since 2011 as an ordained minister of God with a heart for community outreach and a deep commitment to pastoral care. He leads the congregation with humility, compassion, and biblical conviction and stands for the Truth of God's word, preaching and teaching Him with love, sincerity and care.

He earned his Postgraduate Diploma in Theology from The Redeemed Christian Bible College (RCBC) and was ordained as a Reverend in 2022. His ministry focuses on spiritual transformation through the Word of God, worship, and connecting with people from all walks of life. Pastor Godwin is married with children and has authored "Understanding the Holy Spirit," reflecting his passion for teaching biblical truths.

Our Story

God's Vessels International Ministry was founded in 2010 with a simple yet powerful vision: to see every believer equipped as God's vessel for His glory. What started as a small home fellowship of 12 families has grown into a vibrant church community of over 500 members.

Our journey began when Pastor John and Sarah Thompson felt God's calling to plant a church that would emphasize both spiritual growth and community service. Through prayer, dedication, and the support of faithful members, GVIM has become a beacon of hope in our community.

Today, we continue to grow not just in numbers, but in spiritual maturity, community impact, and global outreach. Our heart remains the same: to see lives transformed by the love of Jesus Christ and to equip believers for meaningful service in God's kingdom.

Key Milestones

  • 2010: Church founded with 12 families
  • 2012: First church building acquired
  • 2015: Youth ministry launched
  • 2018: Community outreach center opened
  • 2020: Online ministry and live streaming launched
  • 2023: International missions program established
